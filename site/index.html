Connect your calendars

    1. Copy your calendar link

    1. In Canvas, open Calendar from the left menu.
    2. Click Calendar Feed at the bottom of the right sidebar.
    3. Copy the link it shows. It ends in .ics and covers all your courses.
    1. Open Outlook on the web and go to Settings → Calendar → Shared calendars.
    2. Under Publish a calendar, pick your calendar, choose Can view all details, and click Publish.
    3. Copy the ICS link. If Publish is missing, your school has turned it off; use the file import below.
    1. Classroom puts each class's due dates on its own Google Calendar. Open calendar.google.com on a computer.
    2. Hover the class calendar in the left list, open Settings and sharing, and scroll to Integrate calendar.
    3. Copy the Secret address in iCal format. Repeat for each class. If it is hidden, your school has turned it off; use the file import below.

    2. Save the links in your GitHub repository

    1. In your repository, open Settings → Secrets and variables → Actions.
    2. Add a secret named CALENDAR_FEEDS. Put one calendar per line, as Name | link. For example Canvas | https://…/feeds/calendars/user_abc.ics.
    3. Add a secret named PLANNER_PASSPHRASE with a passphrase of at least 12 characters. You type it here once to unlock your schedule.
    4. Open the Actions tab and run Sync calendars and publish. After that it refreshes every hour on its own.

    Why this way: Canvas, Microsoft and Google do not let a web page read these links directly, so your repository fetches them for you and publishes an encrypted copy that only your passphrase opens. Treat the links like passwords.
